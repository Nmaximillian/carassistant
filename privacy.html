Car Assistant – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 (conversation memory, location services)

Car Assistant is a private, non-commercial voice assistant for a single household's car. It is not offered to the public. This page explains what data it uses and why.

Google account data

When a household member links a Google account, the app requests read-only access to:

  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly): to answer spoken questions such as "what's on my calendar tomorrow?" or "where is my booking?".
  • Gmail (gmail.readonly): to answer spoken questions about the person's own emails, such as booking confirmations.
  • The account's email address (openid, email): to show which account is linked.

The app never creates, changes, sends or deletes calendar events or emails.

How the data is used

  • Calendar and email data is read only when the verified account owner asks a question, and only the items needed for the answer (for example event title, time and location) are used.
  • To speak the answer, those items are passed to the voice AI service (OpenAI Realtime API) for that one conversation. They are not used to train AI models, for advertising, or for any other purpose.
  • Calendar and email content is not stored by Car Assistant's server. So that follow-up questions work ("how far is it from there?"), the phone keeps a short recap of the conversation, including the details that were looked up, in its working memory only: never saved to storage, deleted after a period the user chooses (5 hours by default) or when the app closes, and only ever used for the same person.
  • Google user data is not sold or transferred to anyone else.

What is stored

  • The linked account's email address, a label ("personal" / "work"), and Google's refresh token. The token is encrypted on the household's own server and is used only to read the scopes listed above.
  • Voice profiles (used to recognise who is speaking) stay encrypted on the phone and are never uploaded.
  • Audit records contain no message, calendar or voice content and are deleted after 30 days.

Location

If enabled, the phone's location is sent only with a question that needs it, and is not stored. It is shared with the service that answers that question: the weather services Open-Meteo and Buienradar (rounded to about 1 km), Google Places for nearby places, and the research service for "near me" questions. "Where am I?" is answered on the phone itself.

Your control

  • Unlink an account in the app (Voices → Unlink). This revokes the access at Google and deletes the stored token.
  • You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting a person's profile in the app revokes all of their linked accounts.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Car Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
